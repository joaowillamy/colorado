whatsapp/ project · Amazon Bedrock

WhatsApp virtual agent

A customer sends a message, and an AI answers based on your documents, looks up real data, and does not leak sensitive information. AWS services chained through a queue, with no servers to maintain.

The journey of a message

Example: Diego writes "How much is the VIP ticket?". Move forward with the buttons, the keyboard arrows, or by clicking the services.
